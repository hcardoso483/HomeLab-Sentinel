# HomeLab Sentinel Vision

## Our Mission

HomeLab Sentinel exists to simplify homelab management by automatically discovering, understanding, monitoring, and assisting with the operation of home infrastructure.

The project is built around a simple belief:

> Technology should adapt to the user, not the other way around.

Users should spend their time enjoying and improving their homelab—not manually configuring dozens of tools.

---

# Our Vision

We envision HomeLab Sentinel becoming the central intelligence layer for a homelab.

Rather than replacing existing open-source projects, HomeLab Sentinel connects them into a single, cohesive platform.

The long-term goal is to provide an experience where installation takes minutes, discovery is automatic, monitoring is intelligent, and management feels natural.

---

# Core Principles

## Automatic Discovery

If HomeLab Sentinel can discover something automatically, it should.

Manual configuration should be the exception, not the rule.

---

## Understanding Before Monitoring

Monitoring is more valuable when the platform understands what it is monitoring.

A discovered device should be identified, classified, and assigned the most appropriate monitoring strategy.

---

## Simplicity

Complex systems are acceptable.

Complex user experiences are not.

---

## Modularity

Every major feature should be modular.

Users should be able to enable or disable components without affecting the rest of the platform.

---

## Transparency

Users should always know:

- what HomeLab Sentinel discovered
- why it made a decision
- how recommendations were generated

---

## Community

The project should be welcoming to everyone.

Whether someone contributes code, documentation, translations, testing, bug reports, or ideas, every contribution is valuable.

---

# Long-Term Goals

HomeLab Sentinel should eventually be able to:

- Discover devices automatically
- Build a living inventory
- Detect operating systems and services
- Recommend monitoring strategies
- Deploy monitoring components
- Monitor infrastructure health
- Monitor its own health
- Recommend corrective actions
- Provide one unified dashboard
- Minimize manual configuration

---

# Project Philosophy

HomeLab Sentinel is not just another monitoring dashboard.

It is a platform that discovers, understands, monitors, and assists.

---

# Our Motto

Discover.

Understand.

Monitor.

Assist.

---

This document is intended to guide future development.

Every feature should support this vision.

# What HomeLab Sentinel is NOT

HomeLab Sentinel is not intended to replace:

- Prometheus
- Grafana
- Loki
- Alertmanager
- NetBox
- Portainer

Instead, it integrates the best open-source projects into a unified experience.

Whenever possible, HomeLab Sentinel will reuse proven technologies instead of reinventing them.
